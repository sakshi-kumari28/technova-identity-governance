# 🛡️ TechNova Identity Governance & Administration (IGA) Platform

A comprehensive **Identity Governance & Administration (IGA)** platform inspired by **SailPoint IdentityIQ**, developed using **Spring Boot**, **Spring Data JPA**, and **MySQL**.

This project simulates enterprise Identity and Access Management (IAM) processes including **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**, **Joiner-Mover-Leaver (JML) lifecycle management**, **Access Request workflows**, **Automatic Provisioning & Deprovisioning**, and **Audit Logging**.

---

# 📌 Project Overview

Organizations require centralized identity governance to securely manage employee identities, application accounts, business roles, and access permissions.

This platform provides a simplified enterprise IAM solution capable of:

- Employee Identity Management
- Application Management
- Account Management
- Entitlement Management
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Access Request Workflow
- Joiner-Mover-Leaver (JML) Lifecycle Management
- Automatic Provisioning & Deprovisioning
- Comprehensive Audit Logging

---

# 🚀 Features

## Identity Management

- Employee Management
- Target Application Management
- Account Management
- Entitlement Management

## Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

- Business Role Management
- Role → Entitlement Mapping
- Employee → Business Role Assignment
- Automatic Access Provisioning
- Automatic Access Deprovisioning

## Access Management

- Create Access Requests
- Approve Access Requests
- Reject Access Requests
- Duplicate Request Prevention
- Duplicate Entitlement Prevention

## Lifecycle Management

- JOINER
- MOVER
- LEAVER

Automatically:

- Activate Employees
- Update Departments & Job Titles
- Deactivate Employees
- Disable Accounts
- Remove Entitlements

## Audit & Compliance

- Lifecycle Audit
- Access Request Audit
- Provisioning Audit
- RBAC Audit
- Entity Tracking
